GlowPath

Path puzzle Luminous logic Play in browser

Why play

The Hook

GlowPath turns each board into a clean pathfinding challenge. Start on the pulsing entry tile, swipe through adjacent squares, backtrack when needed, and finish when every active tile glows.

How to play

Your First Run

Press the starting tile, then swipe through unlit neighboring tiles. Holes are blocked, revisiting a lit tile is ignored unless it is the previous square, and a completed route lights the whole stage.

  • Start from the pulsing entry square
  • Swipe through each active tile exactly once
  • Backtrack one step to repair a route without restarting

What matters

What to Watch

The useful move in GlowPath is often the one that preserves exits. Watch corners and narrow bridges before committing, because a bright-looking route can still strand an unlit square.

Controls

Controls

  • Tap the pulsing start tile to begin
  • Swipe or drag into adjacent active tiles
  • Swipe into the previous tile to undo the last step

Player tips

Improve Your Run

  • Trace the forced corners before lighting the middle
  • Use backtracking immediately when a route traps itself
  • Leave branching areas flexible until the path shape is clear

Player guide

GlowPath Strategy Notes

Who GlowPath Is For

GlowPath fits players who like compact logic puzzles with a tactile swipe rhythm. Each stage is short, readable, and built around finding one continuous route through the lit squares.

Reading the Board

Look for dead ends, corners, and narrow connectors before you start moving quickly. Strong routes usually handle constrained tiles first, then use open space to connect the remaining squares.

Using Backtrack

Backtracking is not a penalty. If the route blocks itself, swipe into the previous square, turn off the last tile, and correct the path while the board shape is still fresh.

GlowPath FAQ

What kind of puzzle is GlowPath?

GlowPath is a path puzzle where you light every active square by swiping a continuous route from the starting tile.

Can I undo a move in GlowPath?

Yes. Swipe back into the previous square to turn off the last tile and continue from there.
