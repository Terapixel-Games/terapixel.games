Color Crunch

Swipe-and-merge puzzle Tactile Play in browser

Why play

The Hook

Slide the whole board in one direction. Equal-level tiles merge into the next level, and a successful move adds a new tile. Keep space open so the board has room to move.

How to play

Your First Run

Swipe up, down, left, or right, drag with the left mouse button, or use arrow keys or WASD. For a first merge, find two equal tiles in a row and slide toward that row’s edge so they meet.

  • Slide a 4×4 board in four directions
  • Merge equal-level tiles into higher levels
  • Keep empty spaces available for new tiles

What matters

What to Watch

Each input moves the board as a whole. Plan where equal tiles will meet and where the new tile can leave space for another move.

Controls

Controls

  • Arrow keys or WASD slide the board
  • Swipe on touch screens, or hold the left mouse button and drag, then release

Player tips

Improve Your Run

  • Line up equal-level tiles before sliding
  • Leave empty cells for new tiles
  • Check the whole board before choosing a direction

Player guide

Color Crunch Strategy Notes

Sliding the board

Every swipe moves all tiles toward one edge. Equal tiles that meet combine into a higher-level tile.

Keeping room to merge

Successful moves spawn a new tile. Preserve open cells and plan the next merge before filling the board.

Color Crunch FAQ

How do I control Color Crunch?

Use arrow keys, WASD, a touch swipe, or a left-mouse drag to slide the 4×4 board.

What is the goal?

Merge equal tiles into higher levels while keeping legal moves available.
