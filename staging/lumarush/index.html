LumaRush

Connected-color puzzle Pattern Play in browser

Why play

The Hook

Tap a group of matching tiles connected horizontally or vertically to clear it. Tiles fall into the gaps and the board refills, creating new groups to spot.

How to play

Your First Run

Find at least three matching tiles joined along their edges, then tap or click any tile in that group. For your first move, look for a row of three matching colors and tap one of them; diagonal neighbors do not connect.

  • Clear connected groups of matching colors
  • Watch tiles fall and refill the board
  • Find larger groups before committing

What matters

What to Watch

Read the whole connected group before tapping. Clearing one group changes the neighboring tiles, so check the new board after each move.

Controls

Controls

  • Tap or click any tile in a connected matching group
  • Match at least three tiles connected horizontally or vertically

Player tips

Improve Your Run

  • Look for edge-connected colors rather than diagonal matches
  • Check the board again after tiles fall
  • Compare nearby groups before choosing a move

Player guide

LumaRush Strategy Notes

Reading connected groups

Matching colors connect through shared edges. Start with a group of three or more and tap any member to clear the group.

After a clear

Tiles fall and new tiles refill the gaps. Recheck the new connections before your next tap.
