Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026

Overview

Terapixel Games is committed to responsible data handling. This page explains what information may be collected when you use our website and browser games.

Information We Collect

We may receive limited technical information such as browser type, device details, pages visited, approximate region, referring pages, and basic interaction events through analytics, hosting, and advertising providers.

We do not require sensitive personal information to play. If you email us, we only process the details you choose to share.

Cookies and Advertising

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for performance measurement and to support advertising, including Google AdSense. Third-party providers may use cookies to serve, personalize, limit, and measure ads according to their own policies and user controls.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads based on visits to this site and other sites on the internet.

Visitors may opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings. Visitors may also opt out of some third-party vendors' uses of cookies for personalized advertising through AboutAds.info.

We do not ask visitors to click ads, and ads are kept separate from game controls, navigation, and download or launch links.

Third-Party Services

Some game content is delivered through third-party platforms. Those services may process data under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy as products evolve. Updates are posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@terapixel.games.
