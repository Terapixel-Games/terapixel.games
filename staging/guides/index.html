Tips and Controls

Learn the goal, pick up the controls, and get a few practical tips before your next run. These notes are short on purpose so you can get back to playing.

Path puzzle

GlowPath

GlowPath is a swipe puzzle about lighting every active square from one starting point without wasting the route.

The useful move in GlowPath is often the one that preserves exits. Watch corners and narrow bridges before committing, because a bright-looking route can still strand an unlit square.

  • Trace the forced corners before lighting the middle
  • Use backtracking immediately when a route traps itself
  • Leave branching areas flexible until the path shape is clear

Connected-color puzzle

LumaRush

LumaRush is a tap puzzle about clearing connected groups of matching colors.

Read the whole connected group before tapping. Clearing one group changes the neighboring tiles, so check the new board after each move.

  • Look for edge-connected colors rather than diagonal matches
  • Check the board again after tiles fall
  • Compare nearby groups before choosing a move

Kart racing

Racer

Racer is a browser kart-racing game with toy-scale tracks, quick laps, and multiplayer-ready race sessions.

If you are sliding wide, you probably started the turn too late. Slow down before the corner, aim for a clean exit, and let speed come from rhythm instead of correction.

  • Brake or ease off before sharp turns instead of correcting late
  • Learn one track section at a time before chasing full-run speed
  • Use early laps to find boost timing and cleaner racing lines

Swipe-and-merge puzzle

Color Crunch

Color Crunch is a 4×4 swipe-and-merge puzzle about combining equal tiles into higher levels.

Each input moves the board as a whole. Plan where equal tiles will meet and where the new tile can leave space for another move.

  • Line up equal-level tiles before sliding
  • Leave empty cells for new tiles
  • Check the whole board before choosing a direction

Tunnel brickbreaker

Worm Breaker

Worm Breaker is a cylindrical tunnel brickbreaker with a paddle that rotates around the tunnel wall.

The paddle moves around the tunnel rather than across a flat screen. Watch the ball’s angular position as it returns and adjust early to meet it.

  • Follow the ball around the tunnel wall
  • Adjust the paddle before the ball reaches you
  • Use small corrections to line up a rebound

Card speedrun

SpeedSolitaire

SpeedSolitaire is a fast browser card game that blends classic solitaire logic with time-focused play.

SpeedSolitaire keeps the familiar solitaire structure but frames each session around pace. The best runs come from reducing pauses, recognizing card movement options early, and avoiding unnecessary backtracking.

  • Expose hidden cards as early as possible when the move is clean
  • Do not chase every possible move if it slows the whole run
  • Practice one session goal at a time, such as fewer hesitations or faster foundation moves

Practice

One Thing at a Time

Start With One Skill

Pick one thing to improve before you start: smoother steering, faster color scanning, quicker card choices, or calmer movement under pressure. A focused retry is usually better than a random one.

Use Fullscreen When It Helps

Fullscreen gives the game more room and makes motion easier to read. It is especially useful on smaller screens or when you are trying to beat a score.
