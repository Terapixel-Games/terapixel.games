Worm Breaker

Tunnel brickbreaker Sharp reflex Play in browser

Why play

The Hook

Rotate the glowing paddle to rebound the ball into bricks deeper in the tunnel. Track the returning ball around the cylinder and bring the paddle into its path before it passes you.

How to play

Your First Run

Use Left/Right or A/D to rotate the paddle, or press and drag horizontally on a touch screen. Start by making a small rotation, then track the ball’s return and line up a rebound to send it back toward the bricks.

  • Rotate a paddle around a cylindrical tunnel
  • Rebound the ball into tunnel bricks
  • Track the ball’s angle on its return

What matters

What to Watch

The paddle moves around the tunnel rather than across a flat screen. Watch the ball’s angular position as it returns and adjust early to meet it.

Controls

Controls

  • Left/Right arrow keys or A/D rotate the paddle
  • On touch screens, press and drag horizontally; release to stop steering

Player tips

Improve Your Run

  • Follow the ball around the tunnel wall
  • Adjust the paddle before the ball reaches you
  • Use small corrections to line up a rebound

Player guide

Worm Breaker Strategy Notes

Rotating the paddle

Use Left/Right or A/D, or horizontal touch dragging, to move the paddle around the cylinder.

Returning the ball

Meet the returning ball with the paddle to bounce it into the tunnel bricks. Missing below the paddle loses the ball.

Worm Breaker FAQ

What is the goal in Worm Breaker?

Rebound the ball with a rotating paddle to break bricks inside a cylindrical tunnel.

Which keyboard controls are supported?

Left/Right arrows and A/D rotate the paddle. Touch screens support horizontal dragging.
